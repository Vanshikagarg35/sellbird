TaskNest Software Pvt Ltd - company information (internal doc)

About us
TaskNest is a small software company based in Pune, started in 2021. We are a team of 14 people.
We make simple software for small businesses in India who are still running things on phone calls,
WhatsApp and paper registers.

Products

1. BookEasy - online appointment booking
BookEasy lets clinics, salons and service businesses take bookings online 24x7. Customers book from
a link (can be shared on WhatsApp / Instagram / Google profile). Automatic SMS and WhatsApp reminders
reduce no-shows. Staff calendar, multiple branches, simple reports.
Pricing: Rs 1,499 per month per branch. 14 day free trial.
Current customers: around 120 clinics and salons, mostly in Maharashtra and Karnataka.
Customers tell us they get fewer missed calls and fewer no-shows. One dental clinic said reception
staff saves around 2 hours a day.

2. StockSaathi - inventory and billing for retail shops
StockSaathi is a GST billing + inventory app for small retailers and distributors (hardware stores,
pharmacies, electronics shops). Barcode billing, low stock alerts, supplier orders, works on Android
and desktop, works offline and syncs later.
Pricing: Rs 999 per month, or Rs 9,999 per year.
Current customers: around 300 shops.

What makes us different
- Built for Indian small businesses (Hindi/Marathi UI, GST, WhatsApp)
- Very simple, staff can learn in one day
- Local support on phone in Hindi, English and Marathi
